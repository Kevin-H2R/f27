How to book an immigration appointment on HiKorea

By Kevin Herrbach · Updated 2026-10-21

You cannot walk into a Korean immigration office any more. Every visit needs a reservation on HiKorea, and the slots run out weeks ahead. This is the whole process, including what to do when there is no slot before your deadline.

Which office

The office for your registered address, not the one near your workplace. HiKorea's booking form picks it from your address, and another office will turn you away. If you moved recently, report the address change first; it can be done on HiKorea without a visit.

How far ahead

Expect the first free slot 4 to 6 weeks out in Seoul and Gyeonggi, less in smaller cities. Slots cannot be booked for the same day. Book the appointment before your documents are ready, then collect the documents in the weeks before it.

For an extension of stay you can file from 4 months before the expiry date. So the moment you are inside that window, book.

Booking, step by step

  1. Go to hikorea.go.kr and switch the site to English if you prefer; the booking form is identical.
  2. Click Reserve Visit (Apply), in Korean 방문예약 신청.
  3. Log in as a member, or use non-member identification with your passport number and ARC number. Bookings must be in the applicant's own name.
  4. Pick the office, the service type (new registration, extension, change of status, and so on), the date and the time.
  5. Confirm. Print or screenshot the reservation receipt. The counter asks for it.

One reservation per person per service. Booking two slots to keep options open is blocked by the system and gets both cancelled if noticed.

Cancel properly

Cancel at least a day ahead under 예약현황 조회 (reservation status). No-shows are tracked. A pattern of them can get your account blocked from online booking for a period, which puts you in the walk-in queue that no longer exists.

No slot before your visa expires

This happens, especially in spring and autumn. Options, in order:

  1. Check daily. Cancellations open single slots at random times. Morning checks around 9:00 catch most of them.
  2. Call 1345. The immigration contact center can see slots across the office and sometimes books emergency ones for people whose stay expires before the first open date. Have your ARC number and expiry date ready.
  3. File online instead. Many extensions and some changes of status can be filed through HiKorea's e-application without a visit. If your case qualifies, you avoid the slot problem entirely.
  4. Visit with proof. If your expiry is within days and nothing else worked, go to the office with your passport, ARC and a printout showing no available slots. Offices have a procedure for this. It is slow and not guaranteed, so treat it as the last resort.

Filing an application, online or in person, before the expiry date keeps you legal while it processes, even if the decision comes after the date. Missing the date is the one thing that cannot be fixed cheaply.

On the day

  • Arrive 20 to 30 minutes early. Counters run late, so a 10:00 slot can start at 10:30.
  • Bring the reservation receipt, passport, ARC, photo, and photocopies of every document.
  • The appointment itself takes 10 to 15 minutes if the file is in order. Fees are paid at the bank counter inside the office, by card.
  • You leave with a receipt showing the pickup or decision date. Keep it with your ARC until the new card arrives.
