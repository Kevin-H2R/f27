# Volunteer hours for Korean visa points

Volunteering is a small line on the F-2-7 table and the one most people learn about too late. The bonus is counted in **years of registered activity**, and the clock starts the day your first activity is logged, not the day you started helping.

| Registered volunteering | F-2-7 bonus |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 years | +1 |
| 2 to 3 years | +5 |
| 3 years or more | +7 |

Seven points is the difference between one bracket of income and the next. It costs an afternoon a month. Offices may also look at a minimum number of hours per year, so do not register once and disappear.

## Only two registries count

Immigration does not take a letter from an NGO. It takes a certificate from one of the two national volunteer systems:

- **1365** (1365.go.kr), run by the Ministry of the Interior. Most local and municipal volunteering.
- **VMS** (vms.or.kr), run by the Ministry of Health and Welfare. Social welfare facilities: care homes, disability centers, children's centers.

Both issue a 봉사활동 확인서 with your hours, dates and the organizations. Hours logged in either are merged into one record for government purposes, and many activities are posted on both.

If you already volunteer somewhere, ask the organization whether it logs hours to 1365 or VMS. If it does not, your hours have been invisible. Ask them to register as a 수요처, which takes them a few days, or find a listed activity.

## Signing up as a foreigner

Both sites accept the 외국인등록번호 from your ARC as the ID number. Identity verification goes through a Korean phone number in your name. Sign-up takes ten minutes, in Korean. The English versions of the sites are partial, so keep a translation tool open.

After signing up, search activities by district (구), apply, show up, and the organization confirms your hours within a few days. Check your 실적 page after each activity; unconfirmed hours are the most common gap at the end.

## What activities are available

In Seoul and the big cities: Han river and park cleanups, food banks and soup kitchens, kimchi making in autumn, foreign-language help at community centers, event support. Several listings are in English or specifically recruit foreigners. Weekend morning slots fill a week ahead.

## Getting the certificate

Log in to 1365 or VMS, go to 봉사실적 → 확인서 발급, select the period, print the PDF. It carries a verification number. Print it the week before your immigration appointment so the dates cover everything you did.

## Plan backwards

For the +7 you need three years on the record before you file. If your next F-2-7 renewal is in 18 months, register now and you will carry +1 into that renewal and +5 into the one after. For the F-5-16 permanent residency the same volunteer record counts again.
