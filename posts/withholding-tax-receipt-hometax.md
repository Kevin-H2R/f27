# How to get your 근로소득 원천징수영수증 from Hometax

The 근로소득 원천징수영수증 is the receipt your employer files with the tax office after year-end settlement: what they paid you, what they withheld, what you owe or get back. Immigration asks for it next to the 소득금액증명 and cross-checks the two. Banks ask for it for loans and credit cards.

## Two ways to get it

**From HR.** Your employer can print it any time, for any year, with a company seal. They can also issue a mid-year one if you leave a job, the 중도퇴사 version. This is the fastest route and the only one for the current year.

**From Hometax.** Once the employer has filed, the same document sits in your Hometax account:

1. Log in at hometax.go.kr.
2. Type **지급명세서 등 제출내역** in the search box at the top and open it. Hometax moves menus every year; the search box does not move.
3. Set the income type to **근로소득** and the year, click 조회하기.
4. Click **원천징수영수증 보기** on the row, then save as PDF from the viewer.

Employers must file by 10 March for the previous year. It shows up on Hometax in the weeks after, usually by early April.

## The numbers on it

- **총급여** (box 21): gross salary for the year. The headline number, not the one immigration counts.
- **근로소득금액** (box 23): gross minus the earned-income deduction. This is the figure that appears on the 소득금액증명 as 소득금액 and that the F-2-7 points use. The 총급여 line shows up there as 수입금액.
- **결정세액**: the tax actually due for the year, after credits.
- **차감징수세액**: what you paid in or got back at settlement. Negative means a refund.

## Why immigration wants both

The 소득금액증명 is the tax office's certified statement. The 원천징수영수증 is the employer's filing it was built from. Numbers should match. When they do not, it is usually because you changed jobs mid-year and only one employer did the settlement, or because the current year's receipt is a 중도퇴사 one covering a partial year.

Useful timing detail: last year's 원천징수영수증 exists from mid-March, but last year's 소득금액증명 only exists from 1 May. Some immigration offices accept the receipt as provisional income proof in that gap. Call 1345 and ask before you build a plan on it.

## Freelancers

If you are paid under the 3.3 percent withholding without a 사업자, your version is the 사업소득 원천징수영수증, same screen on Hometax, income type 사업소득. If you invoice through a 사업자등록, there is no receipt; your proof is the May 종합소득세 return and the 소득금액증명 that follows in July.
