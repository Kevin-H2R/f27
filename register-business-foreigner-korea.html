Registering a 개인사업자 as a foreigner

By Kevin Herrbach · Updated 2026-10-21

A 개인사업자 is a sole proprietorship: a business registration number in your own name, no company, no capital, no lawyer. It lets you invoice clients, pay yourself from freelance work, and deduct expenses. Korean clients will ask for it before they pay you. Foreign clients will not, but your Korean bank and the tax office will care.

Who can

  • F-2, F-5, F-6: register freely. No permission from immigration needed. The F-2-7 points visa is in this group, which is one of its main practical benefits.
  • D-8, D-9: business visas, registration is part of the visa itself.
  • E-1 to E-7, D-2, D-10 and the rest: not without a 체류자격외활동허가 (permission for activities outside your status) from immigration first. Registering without it is a visa violation, and the tax office does not check for you.

Before you go

Three decisions, each a one-line answer:

  1. Business address. Your home is fine for a desk business. You need the housing contract (임대차계약서) in your name. If the contract is in someone else's name, you need their written consent, and if it is 월세 some landlords object. A coworking space's 사업자등록용 contract works too.
  2. Industry code (업종코드). Software freelancers use 722000, 소프트웨어 개발 및 공급업, or 749609 for other IT consulting. Pick the one that matches the invoices you will send. You can add codes later.
  3. Tax type. 간이과세자 (simplified) if you expect under about 104 million won of revenue a year and your clients do not need VAT invoices. 일반과세자 (general) if your clients are companies that want 세금계산서. Companies usually do. Exporting services to foreign clients is zero-rated VAT either way, so 일반 costs you nothing extra there and lets you reclaim VAT on a laptop.

Where: Hometax or the counter

Hometax: log in, type 사업자등록 신청 in the search box, follow the form, upload a scan of the housing contract. The certificate usually arrives in Hometax within two business days.

Tax office (세무서): the counter for your business address. Bring your ARC, the housing contract, and know your 업종코드. Fill one form. The officer registers you on the spot.

I registered mine under the F-2-7 at the counter. Went in the morning, had the 사업자등록증 the same afternoon. No fee.

What changes afterwards

  • VAT returns (부가가치세): 일반과세자 file in January and July. 간이과세자 file once in January. Zero revenue still means a return.
  • Income tax (종합소득세): every May, for the previous year. Your freelance income lands on the 소득금액증명 as 사업소득, which is what immigration reads for the F-2-7, so it counts for your points.
  • Health insurance: if you have no employer, you move to 지역가입자 and premiums are assessed on income and assets. If you keep a job and freelance on the side, nothing changes until the side income is large.
  • Bookkeeping: keep a separate bank account for the business from day one. It is not required. It is what makes May bearable.
  • Closing: 폐업신고 on Hometax takes five minutes if you stop. Do it, or the returns keep coming.

For the F-2-7 renewal

Immigration accepts 사업소득 the same as salary. Bring the 사업자등록증, the 소득금액증명, and the May 종합소득세 return receipt. The 1 July availability of the income certificate applies to you, so file renewal after July if last year's income was better than the year before.
