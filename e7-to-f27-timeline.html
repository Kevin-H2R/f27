E-7 to F-2-7: a timeline from your first year

By Kevin Herrbach · Updated 2026-10-21

Most people hear about the F-2-7 in their third year, when a colleague gets one, and then discover that half the points they could have had needed to be started in year one. This is the plan I would follow if I arrived today.

The gate

To file as a professional-track applicant you need 3 consecutive years on E-1 to E-7 or D-5 to D-9. The wait is waived if last year's taxable income was 40 million won or more, or if you hold a Korean degree and qualify under the study-talent track. Points still need to reach 80.

Year one: set up the clocks

  • Register on 1365 or VMS and log volunteer hours. The bonus is counted in years of registered activity. Two afternoons a month from year one is +5 by the time you file and +7 at your first renewal.
  • Take the KIIP placement test. Even if you do nothing with it yet, it tells you which stage you would enter and how far 20 or 30 points are.
  • Order a second apostilled copy of your diploma from home while you still have contacts there. Immigration may keep the first one.
  • Count your points on today's table. Age, degree, and your contract salary minus about 15 percent for the taxable income figure. If that is already 80, you are waiting on the gate, not on points.

Year two: build the big block

  • Korean. Pick a route. KIIP stage 5 is 30 points and takes one or two terms if you place into stage 4 or 5. TOPIK 4 is 15 points in one sitting. Decide by how many points you are short.
  • Income. The 소득금액증명 for year two becomes available on 1 May of year three. If a raise is coming, get it into year two's pay, not year three's.
  • Keep the record clean. Immigration fines of 500,000 won and above and any criminal fine cost 10 to 40 points for 3 to 5 years, and a late ARC address report is the most common way people pick one up.

Year three: file

  • Book the HiKorea slot the day you enter the window. Slots run 4 to 6 weeks out.
  • Wait for 1 May if year two's income was better than year one's, so the newest certificate counts.
  • Assemble the file in the order of the points sheet: diploma, TOPIK or KIIP record, 소득금액증명, 원천징수영수증, contract, employer registration, housing contract, volunteer certificate, photos, forms.
  • File before the 3-year mark passes? No, after. The three years must be complete on the filing date. Book the slot for the first week after your third anniversary.

The two shortcuts

  1. Income of 40 million won taxable in the previous year waives the 3-year stay. A developer on a mid-level Seoul salary often clears it in year two. If you do, you can file in year two with 80 points.
  2. A Korean master's or doctorate opens the study-talent track with its own shorter stay requirement and +7 to +10 bonus points. Not a reason to enroll, but if you are already in a program, it changes your timeline.

What my file looked like

Three years on an E-7, filed at 29 with a STEM master's, no Korean, taxable income in the 40 to 50 million bracket, no volunteer record: 85 points on today's table. I scraped past 80 on the 2024 table with nothing to spare. With the year-one volunteer registration and a KIIP stage I would have had 15 to 35 points of margin and a longer first stay period. Start the clocks in year one.
