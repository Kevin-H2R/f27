      FAQ

      Do I need TOPIK? No. If age, degree and income already reach 80, Korean is optional. It is the cheapest 5 to 20 points for most people though.

      How long must I have stayed? 3 consecutive years on E-1 to E-7 or D-5 to D-9, waived if last year's taxable income was 40 million won or more.

      Which income counts? The taxable income on last year's 소득금액증명 issued by the National Tax Service. Contract salary is not used.

      Do university bonuses stack? No. Only the highest one counts, and all bonuses together cap at 40.
