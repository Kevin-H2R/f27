F-2-7 renewal

By Kevin Herrbach · Updated 2026-10-21

Your first F-2-7 came with 1, 2, 3 or 5 years. Renewal is the same points exercise again, with less drama and a shorter list.

When

You can file from 4 months before the expiry date printed on your ARC, and no later than that date. File early. If your renewal is refused you want time to react while still in status.

Points are recounted

The officer recounts from scratch on the current table, not the one you were approved under. Three things usually change between filings:

  • Age. You may have dropped a bracket. 30 to 34 is 23 points, 35 to 39 is 20, 40 to 44 is 12.
  • Income. Last year's 소득금액증명. Available from 1 May for salaried people and 1 July for 종합소득세 filers. If your income went up, file after that date so the better year counts.
  • Korean. TOPIK expires after 2 years. KIIP completion does not.

Below 80 and still earning at least minimum wage: a one-year grace extension. Below 80 and unemployed: a written pledge buys up to two 6-month extensions, then it is D-10 job-seeker or leave.

Your stay period is set by the higher of total points or income points alone: 1 year for 80 to 109, 2 years for 110 to 119 or income points 40+, 3 years for 120 to 129 or income points 45+, 5 years for 130+ or income points 50+.

Documents

  1. 통합신청서, the integrated application form, from HiKorea.
  2. Passport and ARC.
  3. Passport photo, 3.5 × 4.5 cm, taken within 6 months. A fresh one.
  4. 점수계산표, the points sheet, filled in by you, proofs stapled in the same order.
  5. 소득금액증명 for the most recent processed year.
  6. 근로소득 원천징수영수증 from your employer, or your 종합소득세 return if you file as a freelancer.
  7. Employment contract or 재직증명서, plus the employer's 사업자등록증. Freelancers: your own 사업자등록증 and recent contracts or invoices.
  8. Proof of address: housing contract or 거주사실확인서, original and a photocopy.
  9. TOPIK certificate or KIIP completion record if you claim language points.
  10. Degree certificate. Bring what you have, see below.

Fee: 60,000 won for the extension. If your ARC is reissued at the same time, add 35,000 won.

What got checked at mine

At my first F-2-7 the officer went through every line. At renewal she skipped the overseas criminal record entirely, barely looked at the diploma, and spent her time on the income certificate and the contract. Renewal is about proving you still clear 80. The character checks were done the first time.

Two things she did ask for: photocopies of everything, including the housing contract, so she would not have to copy them herself, and a photo that looked like me this year.

Filing

Book on HiKorea under 방문예약. Slots run 4 to 6 weeks out, so book before the documents are ready. Some F-2 extensions can be filed online through HiKorea's e-application. Check whether yours is offered; many offices still want to see you for the points recount. Processing is 2 to 4 weeks. Your old ARC stays valid during processing, and the receipt you get at the counter proves you filed in time.

Checklist for a boring renewal

  • Filed within 4 months of expiry, after the new 소득금액증명 exists
  • Points recounted on today's table, 80 or more
  • TOPIK still valid on the filing date, or KIIP record attached
  • Every document photocopied once
  • 60,000 won on a card
